## Series identity

- Recurring cast: a Galápagos giant tortoise field botanist with a plain field notebook and a satchel, and a Darwin's finch assistant. Both appear in every image, including the title image, with consistent appearance.
- Setting: Galápagos volcanic islands, black lava rock, vegetation from sparse to lush, the ocean.
- Style: warm storybook natural-history watercolour/gouache; gentle academic humour; natural anatomy.
- Palette: `_brand` palette as accents only. No large purple or orange areas, so that the deck's semantic colours (grey data, purple model, orange focus) stay free.
- Global constraints: no text, letters, numbers, axes, tick marks, charts, logos or watermarks. No image may imply a numerical result or the answer to the opening tenfold-island estimate.
- Format: portrait 3:4 for the current title image (revision T2; the initial landscape T1 is retained in the historical record below); 4:3 or 1:1 for the in-deck images (they sit in a column beside slide text).
- Disclosure on every slide: „Ilustrace vytvořená pomocí AI.“ (`.text-size-tiny .text-right`, produced by `ai_disclosure()`).
